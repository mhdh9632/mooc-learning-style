# Notebook Archive

The archive preserves historical work so earlier reports and stored outputs remain traceable. Archived notebooks are not the recommended execution entry points and their paths have not been normalized.

- `notebooks/baseline_history/` — earlier EDA, preprocessing, and BiLSTM pipelines.
- `notebooks/fslsm_history/` — earlier learning-style experiments and duplicate copies.
- `notebooks/feature_history/` — earlier proportional-feature and clustering work.
- `notes/GEMINI.md` — prior project-context notes.

Three original notebooks had identical normalized source: `fslsm+eventlvl.ipynb`, `Untitled5_fixed.ipynb`, and `Untitled5_fixed (1).ipynb`. One copy is active as `models/fslsm_bilstm/03_all_dimensions_event_level_bilstm.ipynb`; the other two remain here.
